P-Stream v1.8.0

The first full release of P-Stream for Android.

- Movies & shows (Rhea, Zeta) and anime & donghua (Kylie, Sana, Remi), with sources updated automatically in the background
- Sign in with your recovery phrase or a passkey to sync progress, bookmarks, watch history and settings with pstream.cfd
- Watch party, shared with the website
- Downloads, subtitles, multi-audio anime, and "Check for App Update" in Settings

New here? See the install guide: https://github.com/pennythiefuk/P-Stream-Releases#install-p-stream-android
